# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and welcoming; assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and its users.
- Protect privacy: never share other people's or customers' data.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery, trolling, personal or political attacks.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the project maintainers through GitHub's private vulnerability
reporting or by contacting a maintainer directly. All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly,
and the reporter's privacy is respected. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions
and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors whose behavior violates this code.

For the full text, including the enforcement guidelines, see the Contributor Covenant 2.1
linked above.
